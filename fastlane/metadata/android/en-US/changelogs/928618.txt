First Audimmory release: an Android audiobook client for Grimmory, forked from Pageless Mobile. Signs in with your Grimmory username, plays single-file (M4B, M4A, MP3, Opus) and folder-based MP3 audiobooks as one continuous book, downloads for offline listening, and syncs progress and bookmarks with Grimmory's web player. Works with http:// servers on private networks such as Tailscale.
