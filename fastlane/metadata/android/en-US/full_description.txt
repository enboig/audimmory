Audimmory is an Android audiobook player for Grimmory, the self-hosted book server. It is offline-first: browse the audiobooks in your Grimmory libraries, download them for offline listening, play without a connection, and sync your position back to your own server.

You must run your own Grimmory server; the app connects to a server URL that you configure on the login screen. No account or data is sent to any third party.

Grimmory source and setup instructions:
https://github.com/grimmory-tools/grimmory

Features:

• Plays single-file audiobooks (M4B, M4A, MP3, Opus) and folder-based audiobooks made of many MP3 files, as one continuous book.
• Browse your audiobooks by series, shelves, magic shelves, authors, narrators, genres and more.
• Open book details with metadata, chapters, bookmarks, and listening progress.
• Download books (audio and covers) for fully offline playback.
• Media3-powered background playback with notification and lock-screen controls, a mini-player, and a full Now Playing screen.
• Playback progress and bookmarks sync with your Grimmory server, so you can continue in Grimmory's web player.
• Light, dark, and system themes.
